3.2 Trace equivalence

Definition 54 Transition equivalence

def:postset_t,def:TransitionType,def:net,def:preset_t,def:Reversible TrEq The statement of the lemma requires a formal notion of equivalent firing sequences, which is the smallest congruence 1 \(\asymp \) on transition sequences closed under the following rules:

  • \(t;t' ≍ t';t\) if \(t \textsf{ co } t'\),

  • \(t;↽ t ≍ ε\), and

  • \(↽ t;t ≍ ε\).
